The government set the standard through a direction to the Regulator of Social Housing in September 2025. It applies from October 2026 to every registered provider of social housing, and that includes the supported housing they run.

## What the standard requires

All relevant staff must have the skills, knowledge, experience and behaviours to deliver good housing services. Providers must also hold the organisations that deliver services for them, such as managing agents, to the same standard.

## The qualification deadlines

| Role | Qualification | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Senior housing manager | Level 4, regulated by Ofqual | October 2029 for providers with 1,000 or more homes, October 2030 for smaller providers |
| Senior housing executive | Level 5 or a foundation degree | Same as above |

Someone who already holds, or is working towards, an equivalent or higher qualification does not need a new one.

## Who is in scope in supported housing

Homeless Link reports that staff are in scope if managing the delivery of housing management services takes up at least half their time, or is a substantial part of their role. In supported housing that can include scheme managers whose role mixes housing management and support.

A managing agent that delivers housing management for a registered provider can also have staff in scope.

## Who it does not cover

Charities and community interest companies that are not registered providers are outside the standard. Their staff will instead be tested through council reviews and, from 2027, supported housing licensing, which is expected to check that service managers have suitable skills and experience.
